Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

WEIRD MY FACE is a local photo and video face-mixing application. This policy explains what face-related information the app processes, why it processes it, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

Face data and media we process

The app processes only the photos and videos that you choose to capture or select. To align face-mix slices, it also processes face regions and local face-landmark positions, such as the eyes, nose, and mouth. This processing is used only to place, crop, align, and render your face mix. WEIRD MY FACE does not use face data to identify, authenticate, profile, or recognize you or any other person.

How processing works

All face and media processing takes place locally on your iPhone using on-device system technology. WEIRD MY FACE does not operate a server that receives your photos, videos, face images, or face-landmark information for processing.

Local storage and retention

When you save a mix, the app stores the selected source media, generated mix, preview, edit state, and local face-alignment information in the app’s private storage on your device. This information is retained there until you delete that saved project or choose Delete all local app data in Settings. The app does not automatically upload these saved projects to a WEIRD MY FACE cloud service and does not provide a cloud gallery.

Photos or videos that you deliberately save to Apple Photos are controlled by the Apple Photos app. Deleting local app data does not delete those separate exports from Apple Photos.

Deletion and your choices

You can delete an individual saved project in My Mixes. You can also use Delete all local app data in Settings to remove saved projects, their locally stored source media, previews, edit state, and local face-alignment information from this device. You can manage camera and photo-library permission in iOS Settings at any time.

Sharing and third parties

WEIRD MY FACE does not sell, disclose, or share your source media, face images, or face-landmark information with third parties. Saving or sharing an exported result happens only when you choose an Apple system Save or Share action. If you send an export to another app or service, that recipient handles the exported file under its own privacy policy.

No accounts, tracking, or advertising

This version of WEIRD MY FACE has no user accounts, advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not create a public gallery of your content.

In-app purchases

Optional PRO purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes payment information under its own privacy policy. WEIRD MY FACE does not receive your full payment-card details.

Support email

If you contact weirdmyface@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide. We use this only to respond to your inquiry.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests about locally stored app data, email weirdmyface@gmail.com.
