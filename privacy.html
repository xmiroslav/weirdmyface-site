Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

WEIRD MY FACE is a face-mixing application. This policy describes how information is handled when you use the app.

Camera, photos, videos, and face information

The app requests access to the camera or photos you select to enable face mixing. Face processing is performed locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your photos, videos, or face images for processing.

Saved creations

Mixes you choose to save are stored on your device. If you export or share a result, the receiving service may handle it according to its own privacy practices.

In-app purchases

PRO purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes payment information under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Support email

If you contact weirdmyface@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide. We use this to respond to your inquiry.

Permissions and choices

You can manage camera and photo access in iOS Settings. You control which results you save and share. You can delete locally stored data through available app and device controls.

Children

The app is not specifically designed for children. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use and sharing of images.

Changes

We may revise this policy when features or practices change. The effective date will be updated.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email weirdmyface@gmail.com.
