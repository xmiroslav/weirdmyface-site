Frequently asked questions

How do I create a mix?

Capture or import faces, choose the available mixing mode, and combine parts from different faces. Save or share the result using the app controls.

How do I restore PRO?

Use the Restore Purchases option in the app’s purchase or settings screen. Make sure you are signed in to the Apple Account used for the original purchase. If you need help, contact us.

Where are my photos stored?

Face processing is performed on your device. Mixes you save locally remain on your device unless you choose to export or share them.
